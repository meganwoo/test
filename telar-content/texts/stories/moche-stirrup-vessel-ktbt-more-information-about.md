---
title: "More information about the vessel"
---

The way the water flows through the spout and through the body are potentially symbolic of the way fluids flow through humans/living things. Many Moche mediums depict sexual themes and themes reflective of physiological human features. The unique design of the Stirrup spout may be representative of human physical features, the way the water flows puts the design in line with the Moche themes. 

This object in particular is quite small, with the body being about 5x6 inches and the overall size about 8.5x6 inches. Though we can see its current brownish red color, there are also some weathering marks visible which do not allow us to fully analyze the vessel surface with the naked eye. On the body of the vessel there appear to be eight light colored lines that meet at the top of the vessel body and descend to the bottom. Along with these lines there is some iconography depicted, of what is most likely a lizard. Though the lizard figures are present, they are quite difficult to see due to dark weathering patches on the vessel.
